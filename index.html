What is Mission Karmayogi?
A comprehensive capacity-building initiative shifting from 'Rule-based' to 'Role-based' human resource management.

Behavioural Competencies

Inculcates ethics, citizen-centric service delivery, empathy, transparency, and integrity across administrative functions.

Functional Competencies

Equips officers with essential operational capabilities including General Financial Rules (GFR), GeM procurement, and RTI handling.

Domain & Tech Competencies

Specialized domain skills across engineering, healthcare, internal security, taxation, digital infrastructure, and AI in governance.

Core Ecosystem Hubs
Integrated portals designed to facilitate continuous learning, assessment, and career development.

Learn Hub

Role-mapped modules and micro-learning courses available 24x7 across devices.

Career Hub

Direct mapping of competencies with vacancies, deputations, and role appointments.

Network Hub

Peer-to-peer civil servant forums, discussion rooms, and cross-departmental collaboration.

Discuss Hub

Real-time analytics and institutional capacity scoring by the Capacity Building Commission.

Accredited Video Modules
Official e-learning lectures streamable directly inside the platform
